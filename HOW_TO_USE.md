# BLinkMaestra — How to Use (one page)

A DepEd teacher assistant that turns your working documents into DepEd-ready drafts. **Pick a template → answer a few quick questions → get an editable draft you can revise and export.**

---

## 1. Get started (2 min)
1. Open the app and **Create an account** (Full name, Email, Password, Confirm) — or **Sign in** if you already have one.
2. Complete the short **3-step onboarding** (Skips/Continue · everything can be edited later in Settings):
   - **Your role** — Position, School, Division, Region.
   - **What you teach** — Grade level(s), Subject(s), optional Learning competencies.
   - **Preferences** — Language, class duration, teaching preferences.
3. You land on the **Dashboard**, which summarizes your documents and recent work.

## 2. Create a document
1. Go to **Templates** (or press **＋ New work** on the dashboard).
2. **Filter by type** (All / Lesson Plans / Assessments / etc.) and click **Start workflow** on the template you need.
3. In the **workflow**, Maestra auto-fills whatever it already knows from your profile (grade, subject, duration) and asks **only what’s missing**. Use **Browse…** on competency fields to pick from the built-in **DepEd curriculum library**.
4. Press **Generate draft**. (If nothing is missing, it says so and lets you generate right away.)

## 3. During generation
- You’ll see a live **5-stage progress** screen ("Understanding your request → … → Preparing your document") with a timer. This usually takes **1–3 minutes**; you can keep the tab open.
- When done, the document **saves automatically** as **In Progress** and opens in your workspace.

## 4. Review, edit, and refine
- Edit any text directly in the editor — it **autosaves** and keeps **Versions** (view/restore any earlier version).
- **Select text** and use the AI actions — **Improve, Rewrite, Simplify, Expand, Shorten, Translate, Change Tone** — then **Accept change** or **Keep original**.
- **Sources** shows which references were used; watch for anything labeled as an **assumption** and double-check it.
- Mark the final version with **✓ Mark as final** (locks the doc; **Edit anyway · unlock** to change it later).

## 5. Export / manage
- **Export DOCX**, **Export PDF**, or **Print** from the workspace toolbar (exports are rate-limited to save server load).
- **My Documents** — search, sort, filter by status, and use Favorites ★, Archive, and Trash. **AI History** shows every generation and lets you reopen them.

## 6. Billing & subscription
- Every **new account gets 5 free documents** before you’re asked to subscribe. The **Dashboard** and **Settings → Subscription** show how many you have left.
- When your free documents run out, subscribe in **Settings → Subscription** or the paywall: pick **Monthly ₱199** or **Annual ₱1,990**, pay the **GCash number** shown, enter your **reference number**, and click **Submit payment for review**.
- The admin verifies the payment (you’ll get an email) and your access begins/renews **automatically** once approved. Until then your account sits at "pending review" and you can’t generate new documents.

---
**Quick tips:** only official references are used — nothing is invented. Avoid entering learner names (use initials/group descriptions). Not sure about something? Open **Help (?)** in the sidebar for the full searchable help center.
