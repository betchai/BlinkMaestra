Designed around your work

Less repetitive work. More control over what you submit.

BLinkMaestra is not meant to replace teacher judgment. It is designed to reduce the blank-page work around it.

01

Use your saved teaching context

Set your position, school, division, region, grade levels, subjects, language, class duration, and teaching preferences once. Maestra can reuse that context when generating.

02

Choose from the curriculum library

For competency fields, use Browse to select from the built-in curriculum library instead of relying on free-text guessing.

03

Edit, refine, and keep versions

Generated content is a draft. Edit it directly, autosave your changes, and restore earlier versions when you need to.

04

Keep the teacher in control

Review sources, check assumptions, refine selected text, and decide when a document is ready to mark as final.

05

Go beyond lesson plans

Use workflows for assessments, learning materials, school documentation, professional growth, parent communication, classroom management, and school programs.

06

Keep your work organized

My Documents gives you search, sorting, status filters, favorites, archive, and trash. AI History keeps your generated work accessible.
